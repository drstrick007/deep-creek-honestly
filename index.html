The House

Space to spread out, close to everything

Bearly Believable is a 2,850 sq ft, three-floor chalet built for a full house — five bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and a wraparound deck looking out toward the lake and the bridge to McHenry beyond. The house sits across the road from the lake with woods on every other side, five minutes from Wisp Resort and five from Deep Creek Lake State Park. To be straight with you: this is not a lakefront house. There is no private dock, slip, or water access here — what you get is the view, the quiet, and a short drive to the public launches and the state park.

Downstairs, a one-of-a-kind antique bar and game room with a pool table, dartboard, and a 70" TV for movie nights; upstairs, a soaking tub with a forest view. Outside, a six-person hot tub on the covered patio, a gas fire table on the deck, a real wood-burning fire pit out back under genuinely dark sky, and string lights the whole way down the path. Rick and Jen host — guests consistently point to how responsive and accommodating they are throughout the stay.

Theme room · Lower floor

A bar that used to be on a steamboat

The seller’s history was that it came off a New Orleans–to–Baltimore steamboat around 1910, and that the original U-shaped bar had been cut into two L’s. We took our L apart by hand, drove it to Deep Creek, and rebuilt it down here. Pour something and sit at it.

Fires, the grill and the hot tub

Gas fireplace

Runs off the thermostat on the wall to the left of it — that's the only control you need. It can take a minute to catch. The glass gets extremely hot; keep children away from it. Turn the wall thermostat off when you leave.

Wood fire pit

Round the back, and yes, you can use it. Kindling is usually there for the gathering in the yard or up the hill behind the house. Firewood is sold along Garrett Highway — Arrowhead Market and Shop ’n Save both carry it.

The grill

The starter can be temperamental; there's a long lighter in the kitchen. Start low, check the grease tray first, keep it on its mat and clear of the deck railing. Gas off when you're done, cover on once it's cooled.

Hot tub

Three jet buttons, an LED light, and up/down for temperature. The neck-massage seat is the good one. Cover closed after use, and no glass anywhere near it. A service team may stop by during your stay — that's normal.

Trash, bears and the practical bits

Trash & bears

Bagged and tied, into the bear-proof bins inside the wooden enclosure at the bottom of the driveway, doors closed behind you. Nothing left outside. Black bears are real here and unsecured trash brings them in fast. Pickup is early Monday.

Getting in

Two ways: the main entry on the upper deck at the side of the house, and the lower-level French doors. Access details come with your check-in instructions — we don't publish them here.

Garage

Owner storage, not available to guests. Park in the driveway — it's free and there's plenty of room.

Wi-Fi

Yes, including 5G. The network and password are in your check-in instructions.

The white binder

Before you leave

Kitchen

Dishes and pans washed and put away — please don't leave the dishwasher full, the cleaning team has no turnaround time to unload it. Bar glasses through the dishwasher and back under the bar. Clear the fridge and freezer. Coffee maker off and unplugged, toaster unplugged.

Beds & towels

Leave the sheets on any beds you used and leave the beds unmade. That's right — do not make the beds ;-) Bath towels in a pile on the bathroom floor. Beach and hot-tub towels are a courtesy rather than part of the cleaning contract: wash, fold, and back on the rack in the basement laundry room.

House

Thermostat to 62°F in winter or 82°F in summer. Lights and bedroom fans off. Stove, oven and fireplaces off — the gas fireplace thermostat to OFF, since it can keep making low heat with no visible flame. Windows closed and locked.

Outside & out

Trash into the enclosure. Hot tub down to 100°F, lock secured, cover closed. Out through the front door or the basement French doors — if you use the French doors, the key goes back into the combination lock. Exterior doors locked behind you.

Before You Arrive

House Rules & More

Read the house rules

Pets

Dog-friendly — $100 pet fee per dog per stay, 2-dog maximum. A collapsible crate and dog bowls are provided. Let your host know your dog's size/breed at booking for approval. The yard is fertilized, so please clean up after dogs.

Smoking

Strict no-smoking policy anywhere on the property. Evidence of smoking may result in a $500 fee for outdoor cleanup and smell remediation.

Winter Driving

AWD or 4WD is strongly recommended for winter conditions and the driveway.

Indoor Fireplaces

Two indoor fireplaces that work: one in the main living room, one in the theme room downstairs.

Garage

The garage is not available for guest use.

Group Age & Size

The booking guest needs to be 28 or older. Groups over 10 are better suited to families with kids, given the bunk-bed layout — the house does not comfortably accommodate 12 adults, and we would rather say so now than have you find out on arrival.

Quiet Hours

11 PM to 8 AM.
