The House

Space to spread out, close to everything

Bearly Believable is a 2,850 sq ft, 4-floor chalet built for a full house — five bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and a wraparound deck looking out toward the lake and "the bridge to McHenry" beyond. The house sits across the street from the lake, woods on every other side — steps from the water, not on top of it, five minutes from Wisp Resort and five from Deep Creek Lake State Park.

Downstairs, a one-of-a-kind antique bar and game room with a pool table, dartboard, and a 70" TV for movie nights; upstairs, a soaking tub with a forest view. Outside, a six-person hot tub overlooking the lake, a real wood-burning fire pit out back under genuinely dark sky (a new gas fire feature is on the way for the patio), and string lights the whole way down the path. Rick and Jen host — guests consistently point to how responsive and accommodating they are throughout the stay.

Before You Arrive

House Rules & More

Pets

Dog-friendly — $100 pet fee per dog per stay, 2-dog maximum. A collapsible crate and dog bowls are provided. Let your host know your dog's size/breed at booking for approval. The yard is fertilized, so please clean up after dogs.

Smoking

Strict no-smoking policy anywhere on the property. Evidence of smoking may result in a $500 fee for outdoor cleanup and smell remediation.

Winter Driving

AWD or 4WD is strongly recommended for winter conditions and the driveway.

Indoor Fireplaces

Real, working fireplaces — a current amenity, not just for show.

Garage

The garage is not available for guest use.

Group Age & Size

No groups under age 28. Groups over 10 are better suited to families with kids, given the bunk-bed layout — the house does not comfortably accommodate 12 adults.

Quiet Hours

11 PM to 8 AM.

Good to Know Before You Stay

Safety & Property

Garage Roof

Please don't let kids or teens climb the rear garage roof.

Lake & Garrett Highway

The lake and Garrett Highway are both nearby — please use caution with dogs and young kids.

Security Cameras

Exterior cameras cover the driveway and yard only. There is zero camera view of the hot tub.

Alarms

CO alarm and smoke alarm both installed.

Noise & Stairs

Some noise carries between levels, and stairs are required to move between them.

Two Entrances

A ground-floor main entrance, plus an upper entrance via the stairs to the left, leading to the front door on the second balcony level.

The Layout

Room by room

Five bedrooms, three full bathrooms, and a house built for a full group to actually spread out.

Bedroom 1 · Upper floor

The primary suite, under the gable

The whole peak of the house is one room: cathedral ceiling, hanging lights, and triangular windows that fill with blue at dusk and with trees in the morning. Queen bed, its own sitting chair, and room to actually walk around.

En-suite · Bathroom 1

Jacuzzi tub, steps from the bed

The primary bathroom opens straight into the bedroom — tiled soaking tub, for the evening after a day on the mountain.

Bedroom 5 · Lower floor

The bunk room

Bunk beds plus a second full bed, a little desk in the corner, and a sign on the wall that reads Don’t Wake The Bear. This is the room kids ask for.

Theme room · Lower floor

A bar that used to be on a steamboat

This was part of the bar on a steamboat that ran from New Orleans to Baltimore Harbor in 1910. An antique dealer in Amish country in eastern Maryland got hold of it and cut the U-shaped bar into two L’s. One of them is here, and you’re welcome to it.

Theme room · Games

Pool table, darts, and the couch

Full-size pool table, darts, board games, and enough sofa for everyone who isn’t playing. Dogs are welcome here, and they know it.

Theme room · Evenings

70″ TV and the fire going

The other end of the same room: big screen, fireplace lit, cards on the trunk. Where the group ends up after dinner.

Bedroom 3 · The green room

The one with the real desk

A proper desk and a printer, away from the noise — the room to take the call in if someone has to work for an hour.

Bedroom 2

Quilts, and a bear in a rocking chair

There are bears all over this house — in pictures, on shelves, tucked into corners. Families have turned finding them into a game. We’re not telling you where they all are.

Outside · Winter

Arriving in the snow

Wisp is close, the driveway gets plowed, and the house looks like this when you pull in after dark.

Also: full kitchen, dining area, living room, balcony, patio with the hot tub, three full bathrooms, and laundry.
