The House

Space to spread out, close to everything

Bearly Believable is a 2,850 sq ft, 4-floor chalet built for a full house — five bedrooms, four bathrooms, and a wraparound deck looking down toward the lake and the mountain beyond. It's a minute from Deep Creek Lake, five minutes from Wisp Resort, and five minutes from Deep Creek Lake State Park.

Downstairs, a green-walled game room with a pool table and dartboard; upstairs, a soaking tub with a forest view. Outside, a six-person hot tub, a fire pit under genuinely dark sky, and string lights the whole way down the path.
